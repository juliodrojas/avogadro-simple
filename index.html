Simple mode of Avogadro — the free, open-source molecule editor for computational chemistry, molecular modeling, bioinformatics and materials science. Tool names and workflows follow the official Avogadro manual (Navigate, Draw, Bond, Measure, Erase). Colors are standard CPK colors; bond rules follow each element's valence, so an atom can never hold more bonds than chemistry allows. XYZ files made here open in desktop Avogadro.
